# SkyRoute Privacy Policy

SkyRoute is open-source software licensed under GPL-3.0. It does not operate
any proxy, VPN, analytics, advertising, or telemetry service.

## Data we do not collect

SkyRoute does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There is no
account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting
service in this project.

## Data processed on your device

- **Flight plans / profiles**: subscription URLs, configuration files, and
  provider data are stored locally in the app's private storage.
- **Network traffic**: when you start the VPN/TUN tunnel, packets are processed
  locally by the bundled mihomo kernel. They are not sent to the SkyRoute
  project.
- **Logs**: the black box / flight recorder stores logs locally. You can clear
  them at any time.

## Network requests

SkyRoute only makes network requests that you explicitly configure, such as
downloading a subscription, updating a provider, or opening a documentation
link in your browser.

## Permissions

SkyRoute requests VPN, network-state, notification, boot, and app-list
permissions solely to provide its local tunnel and per-app routing features.

## Children

SkyRoute does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Changes to this policy will be published in this repository.
