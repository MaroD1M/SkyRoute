SkyRoute turns every connection into a flight. Origin and destination airports,
animated airspace radar, flight plans, air routes, airlines, a black box and a
control tower: all powered by the mihomo proxy kernel.

Features:
- Rule-based routing, TUN/VPN, DNS, proxies and providers from mihomo
- Live flight radar HUD with animated aircraft
- Flight plans (profiles), air routes (proxy groups), airlines (providers)
- Per-app passenger manifest, airspace control and night-cockpit theme
- Three launcher icons and three cockpit accent styles, switchable in Settings
- Free software, GPL-3.0, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry
